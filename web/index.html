How Room reads a script

  1. Read. Gemini finds every reference and names the job it does in the line.
  2. Match. Qloo search turns each one into an entity in its taste graph.
  3. Map. One Qloo heatmap covers your cities and five anchor cities, so every city shares one scale.
  4. Score. Known is the reference's signal in a city against a household name of the same kind. Loved is its median affinity there.
  5. Swap. For a weak city, Qloo offers what that audience loves, Gemini keeps the ones that do the same job, and Room scores each one again.
